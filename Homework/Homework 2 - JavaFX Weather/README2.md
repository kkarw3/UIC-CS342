### Visual Encodings
* **Bar Chart**: Compares the real-time numeric temperature of Chicago, Los Angeles, and New York on a shared Y-axis.
* **Temperature Progress Bar**: Acts as a gauge mapping 0°F to 100°F onto the 0.0 to 1.0 progress scale. Temperatures below 0°F are clamped to 0.0 (empty), and temperatures above 100°F are clamped to 1.0 (full).
